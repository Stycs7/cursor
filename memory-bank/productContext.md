# Product context

Shearwater Victoria Falls hosts guests from arrival to departure: transfers, Explorers Village, restaurants, and owned activities on the Zambezi, the bridge, and nearby reserves.

The existing public site is busy and promotional. This WordPress design should feel calmer and more editorial — spray, gorge, and lodge — while still making it obvious how to plan a stay.

Guests want: what to do, where to sleep, what to eat, who to ask. Staff want: pages they can edit in WordPress without a developer.
